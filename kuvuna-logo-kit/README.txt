NOTES
- The dark-background PNGs and SVGs are transparent with light-coloured text. They will look
  blank on a white page. Always place them on Midnight Navy or another dark ground.
- Keep a clear margin of one square (the size of one of the mark's squares) around the logo.
- Do not recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to the logo.

FAVICON SETUP (paste in the <head> of your website)
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="48x48">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">

Upload favicon.ico, favicon.svg and apple-touch-icon.png to the root of your site.
icon-192.png and icon-512.png are for a web app manifest (Android home screen icons).
Website builders such as Wix, Shopify, WordPress and Squarespace take favicon-48x48.png or icon-512.png in their settings.
